# Managed Chatbot Service Model

## Integrate Once, Improve Continuously

- Your team connects your website/app to our chatbot once.
- After go-live, future chatbot improvements do not require repeated website development.

## We Manage Behavior Centrally

- Tone, response style, domain behavior, and quality tuning are managed in our chatbot platform.
- Your team does not need to add new admin features in your website for each change.

## Fast Updates Without App Releases

- Requested updates are applied by our team and can go live quickly.
- No full website release cycle is needed for routine chatbot adjustments.

## Safer Rollout Approach

- Changes are applied in a controlled way, validated, and expanded safely.
- If needed, we can quickly roll back to a previous stable behavior.

## Your Control, Your Governance

- You approve desired behavior outcomes.
- We execute and monitor, with clear accountability and support.

## Ongoing Support Included

- We monitor quality and reliability.
- We provide structured support for tuning requests and issue resolution.

## Client Promise

Integrate once. We handle chatbot evolution so your team can focus on your core product.
